Walaa's Sweet Match Guide

This game was handcrafted specially for Walaa. Match colorful candies, make explosive sweet combinations, and spread love across every stage!

🍬 Special Candies & Combos:

  • Match 4 in a line: Creates a Striped Candy that clears an entire row or column!

  • Match 5 in a row: Creates the Walaa Love Bomb Donut! Swap with any color to clear ALL of them!
  • Swap Two Special Candies: Triggers super explosions, double line beams, and full-board clears!

💖 Controls:

Click/tap a candy and click/tap an adjacent candy to swap, or simply drag and swipe smoothly!
